Our goal is to educate users on the various ways to try Minecraft for free, either through the official trials offered by the game's creators, or through custom made modifications by community members. Since the game is widely searched for online, our site serves as a resource for those seeking to get a taste of the game before buying it.


About Minecraft

Minecraft is an international best selling game originally made by Markus "Notch" Persson in 2009. Owned by Mojang AB, its open-ended and creative gameplay is embraced by millions of gamers worldwide. In fact, YouTube's Minecraft content has over 200 billion views! Due to its retro style graphics and low hardware requirements, the game is easily playable and is available across many platforms. While the game may appear to be simple, Minecraft can actually become very complex, especially with the use of mods and redstone circuits. After Microsoft acquired Minecraft in 2014, the sensational game grew even more popular over the years with additional titles such as Story Mode.
